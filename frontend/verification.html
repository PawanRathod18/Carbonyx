Independent review

Verification and minting

Acting as the independent verifier, review each project's quantification report, then approve or reject it. Approved projects can mint CBX credits.

The verifier role is granted on-chain by the platform admin. When you approve a project, the SHA-256 hash of its quantification report is written to the registry contract - permanent, auditable evidence linking the credits to the analysis behind them.
