Our services

What does CARBONYX do?

One platform for the complete blue carbon lifecycle — from raw satellite pixels to a tradable, retirement-grade digital asset. Every number is computed by an open engine, every decision is recorded on-chain.

Satellite quantification

Multispectral Sentinel-2 data is classified with NDVI / NDWI indices per ecosystem, then an InVEST-style coastal blue carbon model estimates biomass, soil carbon and 20-year sequestration.

On-chain verification

An independent verifier reviews each report. Approving a project anchors the report's SHA-256 hash on the blockchain — tamper-proof evidence anyone can audit, forever.

Credit minting

Verified projects mint CBX credits, one credit being exactly one tonne of CO₂-equivalent. A conservative buffer is held back and minting is gated by the smart contract — not by trust.

Transparent trading

A peer-to-peer marketplace with escrow, a 1% platform fee and permanent retirement. Every listing, purchase and retirement is an on-chain event visible in the Chain Log.

The MRV pipeline

How it works

Five steps from a patch of coastline to a retirement-grade carbon credit — each one observable, each one on-chain.

Start now →

01Register

A project owner registers a coastal project with its geographic bounding box on the registry contract.

02Quantify

The engine classifies vegetation from satellite bands and models carbon stock and sequestration.

03Verify

An independent verifier approves or rejects the report; approval anchors the report hash on-chain.

04Mint

CBX credits are minted to the owner, capped by the buffered projection of future sequestration.

05Trade and retire

Credits are listed, bought and finally retired — burned on-chain so they can never be sold again.
