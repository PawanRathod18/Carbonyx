The problem we chose

Coastal ecosystems - mangroves, seagrass meadows and salt marshes - capture carbon at rates several times higher than terrestrial forests, yet blue carbon projects remain locked out of serious climate finance. Current carbon markets suffer from unverifiable claims, opaque trading and expensive auditing. Buyers cannot see where a credit came from; project owners cannot prove their carbon is real.

Our answer

CARBONYX fuses three worlds into one pipeline. Satellite remote sensing (Sentinel-2 + Google Earth Engine) measures the carbon. An InVEST-style model converts pixels into tonnes. A blockchain registry makes every step immutable - verification hashes, credit mints, trades and permanent retirements all live on-chain, auditable by anyone. The result is a blue carbon credit you can actually trust.

What we built

Quantification engine - a synthetic Sentinel-2 simulator (deterministic, offline) plus a real Google Earth Engine client, NDVI/NDWI classification and coastal blue carbon modelling with a machine-learning cross-check.

Three Solidity contracts - a project registry with independent verifier roles, an ERC20-style credit token (1 CBX = 1 tCO2e) with gated minting, and an escrow marketplace priced in ETH with a 1% platform fee and permanent retirement.

Full web platform - a FastAPI backend with SQLite persistence, ten animated pages including an offline world map, analytics charts, per-account portfolios, printable retirement certificates and a guided tour for demos.

Honest engineering - carbon coefficients are documented literature-range defaults, the ML model reports its own R2, and every risk flag the system raises is shown to the verifier - nothing is hidden behind a nice dashboard.
