Methodology and architecture

How CARBONYX computes, verifies and trades blue carbon - the complete methodology, formulas and references behind the platform.

Methodology

1. Satellite classification (Sentinel-2)

The quantification engine classifies vegetation from multispectral imagery using two standard indices:

NDVI = (NIR - RED) / (NIR + RED)   |   NDWI = (GREEN - NIR) / (GREEN + NIR)

Pixels with NDVI above the ecosystem-specific threshold (mangrove 0.45, salt marsh 0.35, seagrass 0.15 - submerged canopies give a weaker signal) and below cloud cover are classified as ecosystem vegetation. In simulated mode, deterministic synthetic Sentinel-2 tiles are generated per project; in real mode, a Google Earth Engine median composite over the COPERNICUS/S2_SR_HARMONIZED collection is used.

2. InVEST-style coastal blue carbon model

Carbon stock follows the Natural Capital Project's Coastal Blue Carbon logic:

stock = AGB + BGB + soil   where   AGB = vigour x max_AGB x area

Above-ground biomass is scaled by a normalised vigour index derived from NDVI; below-ground biomass applies an ecosystem root:shoot ratio; soil carbon applies a per-hectare default over one metre of depth. Sequestration is projected linearly over 20 years with a 10% conservative buffer, mirroring registry buffer pools.

mangrove : max AGB 380 t/ha - soil 550 tC/ha - sequestration 1.80 tC/ha/yr seagrass : max AGB 8 t/ha - soil 110 tC/ha - sequestration 0.80 tC/ha/yr saltmarsh: max AGB 60 t/ha - soil 255 tC/ha - sequestration 1.20 tC/ha/yr 1 tC = 3.667 tCO2e (44/12) - biomass carbon fraction 0.47

3. Machine-learning cross-check

A RandomForest regressor trained on synthetic samples predicts above-ground biomass density from mean NDVI, vegetation fraction and ecosystem type. The model's estimate is compared with the empirical estimate; large deviations raise a risk flag for the verifier. Model quality (R2 on a held-out split) is reported with every estimate and on the Analytics page.

4. Risk flags and recommendation

Rule-based anomaly detection runs over every report: cloud fraction, implausible vegetation fractions, NDVI ceilings, low confidence and ML deviation. The system then issues an explicit recommendation (approve / review / reject) that the human verifier can accept or override.

5. On-chain anchoring

Each report is serialised canonically and hashed with SHA-256. When a verifier approves a project, the hash is written to the registry contract. Minting of CBX credits (1 CBX = 1 tCO2e) is gated by the smart contract: only verified projects, only once, only by the recorded owner, capped by the buffered projection.
